CATHARSIS (2021 Mix)

Single Vibey Ambient

Release Date: July 27th, 2021

Artist / Producer: Tim Mills ☆

Synopsis: CATHARSIS was the first FL project I was fondly proud of. It reminds me of a much, much simpler time. I remember everything. it was 7 AM on a real cloudy, chill day, and I was playing Mario Kart 8 on my Nintendo Switch... Flow-state with it... The mix is loud. I know. I didn't know what I was doing at the time. I had no idea. and you know what, I still kinda don't and that's okay.
