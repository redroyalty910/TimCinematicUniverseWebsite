Tim General Update #5

Posted on September 4th, 2026 @ 6:05 pm EST

WELCOME to TIMCINEMATICUNIVERSE.COM Version 2!

I have a few things to speak, it feels like I've lived seven different lives in the last month, so gather around and pay close attention to what ol' tim has to say...

I really haven't been focusing on much of what I said I would since my last blog post. I had told myself that I would deliver a completed game project at some point during the month of September. Well, that goal is nearly impossible at this point in time. And hey, I know, I remember everything that I said about giving myself a deadline and all that responsible jargon, okay, I know. But life happened. Life happened fast. Fast and un-predictably, as some things in life naturally are.

On the most random sunday of all time, I was out of state with my girlfriend and a bunch of friends, and my job shut down completely out of nowhere, without any warning whatsoever . It was all so sudden, and I was deeply upset, because I was doing really well in there. I found out about the shut-down through an ex-coworker from, basically, an entire different lifetime ago. I found it so strange that none of my managers had contacted me about the shutdown, and it really threw my rhythym into orbit. So, naturally, I dedicated the last month to getting my life back into orbit. During this time, I had many epiphanies about my future as an adult, and, most importantly, a citizen of labor. you know, whatever that entails. I found it difficult to focus on development, and I found myself prioritizing landing another job, and using the remaining time I had with my loved ones instead of the keyboard.

I decided that there is no point in rushing Morrisworm, especially not with the various ambitious concepts that I had wanted to add, I could really make this into a fun little project if given the time. What I would like to do, is have the game fleshed out by the end of the year, hopefully with a playable demo at some point in-between, but that is a strong maybe. It's an ambitious project, but not impossibly ambitious, and the scope is lessened significantly by the pre-existing prototype. So long as I can properly communicate with Thomas, I don't see why I wouldn't be able to organize this project effectively by the end of the year.

Like yeah, I'm back in school and all that, but I'm still motivated to complete this project. it's still something that I really want to complete before I tackle a massive project like EGODEATH or something. I have other ideas too that I'm really eager about, but I might be a little too ambitious for my own good, to a point where I get too far ahead of myself without gauging the scope of the task properly (flaw). But hey! it's all love. I learned a lot in August. Oh! and there was another huge thing that happened behind the scenes! I am deformed in a very funny way! I got an MRI done and learned a lot about myself. So that was a whole other thing... Your favorite tim is kind of broken, heh!

Otherwise, I have been in decent spirits. kayleigh keeps me really happy and hopeful, even if I find myself kind of lost at times, she's always there to light the dimming torches. A person like that is very important to the eco-system of easily dimmable torches. I've been playing Persona 5 in her presence a lot, why is this the longest game ever made?

Enjoy version two of the website, everybody. It did nothing but optimize what you see with your eyeballs. I took various notes on the first version for the last few months, and I did what I could to make it ever-so-slightly better for all of us. So I hope that it looks a little better. Oh! and hey, I have since found a new job, so, I'm okay :) I think I have the chance to make even more money than I did at the last place! cheers everyone, much love. Who else is excited for the fall? I kind of am! :) Last fall was kind of weird, but this will be the ONE!

For the next month or so, I plan on dedicating my free time towards development, with the bets pacing that I could manage. Thank you! Love to all, to everything, more, and then some :)

Oh, and im uh, im 24 in less than a week. Guys.... How did this happen?
