☆ TIM MILLS ☆

Storyteller • Game Dev • Musician • Writer / Poet • Programmer • Hooper • Video Editor

As a young man hailing from the dazzling city of NEW YORK, I shape the world around me with all the stars in the sky on my sleeve. I'm an everything-guy; I find myself enjoying VARIOUS mediums of creation. In my free time, I like to hang out with my incredibly lovely friends, appraise various beers and wines, stream on twitch, build interesting personal programs, develop video games, compose weird music, write suspicious poetry, and play pickup basketball!

Anytime somebody tells me that they're bored, I always respond with... "Well, hey! It sounds like you could use a new hobby!" Because, truly, there are too many interesting and entertaining hobbies for us to choose from! I haven't been bored in 24 years now... and counting! And really, the truth to that, is that I find tremendous joy in art. I think it's the reason that I'm alive, and I don't think we'd be living a life worth living without it!

As a creator who deeply values quality projects, I am far beyond the point of serious regarding my artistic vision. I have a tendency to obsess over even the smallest details, and I revisit specific things that probably never even needed revisiting. With that information in mind, I truly do apologize if I take a while to produce the most of my results... My own perfectionism will 100% be the death of me, but until then, I dream of making some pretty neat stuff! At the end of the day, I'm just some guy who really, really loves to create things. I have a lot of ideas, a lot of love to give, and hopefully a whole lot of time to... do all of those things that I think I want to do!
