☆ SHORT SUMMARY ☆

Hello! (WAVE BACK AT YOUR SCREEN), I'm Tim Mills ☆ - a silly 23-year-old creator of several things that you can find all around this website! Though I primarily describe myself as a "game-developer with sauce," I am creatively versatile.
